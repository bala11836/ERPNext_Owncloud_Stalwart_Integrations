GET READY BEFORE OPENING THE LINK

Use Apple Passwords/iCloud Keychain or Bitwarden Password Manager. Google Password Manager and other providers are not accepted.

Bitwarden:
* Create an account at https://vault.vaultwarden.com/, preferably using the same email address as your Cloud username.
* Install the official phone app or browser extension: https://bitwarden.com/download/
* Before signing in to the installed app or extension, change the server to Self-hosted and enter https://vault.vaultwarden.com. Sign in using the account you just created.
* Set up biometric unlock for convenient unlocking. You may still need your master password when signing in again.
* On a phone, enable Bitwarden in your device's AutoFill/password and passkey settings.
* Avoid installing the Bitwarden browser extension on shared computers.

Apple Passwords: On your iPhone, iPad or Mac, enable iCloud Passwords & Keychain syncing in your Apple Account's iCloud settings. Have your device passcode or Touch ID/Face ID ready.
Setup: https://support.apple.com/en-us/109016

REGISTER YOUR PASSKEY

1. Once your password manager is ready, open the invitation link below.
2. Confirm the page is on sso.example.com. This personal link verifies your email; no second email is required.
3. When asked to create a passkey, save it in Bitwarden or Apple Passwords/iCloud Keychain. Chrome is fine when Bitwarden is the save location. Do not choose Google Password Manager.
4. Confirm with your fingerprint, face or device PIN when prompted, and finish the on-screen steps. Keep access to your password manager for future sign-ins.

If neither approved provider appears, stop and contact your administrator. Never share your master password, device PIN or passkey.
